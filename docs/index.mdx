gitmatter is a tool for legal teams. It helps you review contracts, pull information
out of documents, draft new documents, and ask questions about your files. An AI
assistant can help with all of this, or you can do the work yourself.

Use the audit history to inspect recorded changes by people and connected agents,
including authors, timestamps, messages, and diffs.

## What you can do

- **Review contracts.** gitmatter suggests edits as tracked changes, the same way "track
  changes" works in a word processor. You read each suggestion and decide to accept it or
  reject it.
- **Pull information into a table.** Ask gitmatter to read a stack of documents and fill
  in a table for you — for example, the governing law, the term length, and the renewal
  date of every vendor contract — then export that table.
- **Draft documents.** gitmatter can create a Word document for you, following a set of
  steps you have saved.
- **Ask questions.** Chat about your documents in plain language. The answers point you
  to the exact place in the source document they came from, so you can check them.
- **Use your own AI assistant.** If you already use a tool like Claude or ChatGPT, you
  can connect it to gitmatter and use its tools. Review the resulting changes in the
  audit history.

## Review responsibilities

Check generated drafts, suggested edits, and cited sources before using the result. A
lawyer decides what to accept; a recorded AI action does not establish legal correctness
or replace the practitioner's review.
