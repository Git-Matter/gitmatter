Start by completing a fictional matter, rather than uploading client material during an initial demonstration. The aim is to understand the workflow, what leaves the application, how you check the result, and how the work returns to the matter file.

## Choose a setup path

- **Hosted walkthrough:** [book a walkthrough](https://www.cal.eu/gitmatter/demo) to discuss your recurring task, AI configuration, and assisted setup. No meeting is booked merely by opening that link.
- **Self-hosted:** follow the [self-hosting guide](/admin/self-hosting). The application is MIT-licensed; infrastructure, recovery, and provider-account administration remain your responsibility.

## Work through the checklist

1. **Choose your AI path.** In Settings, connect your practice's approved external agent, or configure an API-provider account for built-in features. AI subscription/API charges may be separate from hosted service charges.
2. **Verify handling for that account.** Read the current terms, settings, retention and training conditions, access permissions, and processing locations. A paid consumer subscription or an API key does not establish suitability for confidential material. See [AI data handling](/admin/ai-data-handling).
3. **Check storage separately.** A document-storage region does not establish where extracted text, account data, logs, backups, or AI processing reside. See [privacy evidence](/admin/privacy-evidence).
4. **Set practice preferences.** In Settings, save your practice display name, correspondence header, preferred sign-off, correspondence instructions, and Australian jurisdiction. These are preferences, not a verified legal identity or approved precedent.
5. **Try the fictional sample.** Open the assistant and choose one of the guided starting actions. Inspect the source documents and prepared instructions before starting a model call.
6. **Check the handoff.** Open source references, check supported facts and uncertainties, inspect a Word draft, and understand the distinction between recorded AI actions and your own document review.

## Before working with client material

Confirm the particular use is authorised and appropriate for the matter, your professional obligations, and the selected services. Use your judgment to assess each draft. Extracted dates need verification and are not automatic legal-deadline calculations.

Where you record a practitioner review, check the exact document version and scope. An AI action or accepted edit is not evidence of your personal review, and later document changes may make an earlier review stale.

## Ask for help

[Book a walkthrough](https://www.cal.eu/gitmatter/demo) or email [contact@gitmatter.com](mailto:contact@gitmatter.com) with your practice role/size, state or territory, the type of recurring task, current tools, and preferred contact details. Do not include client names, documents, confidential details, API keys, passwords, or other credentials. Add credentials only through the designated settings.

The [Australian practitioner pilot](https://gitmatter.com/australian-practices) explains the four-week proposal and AUD quote path. Participation, scope, price, GST treatment, and document handling are agreed individually.
