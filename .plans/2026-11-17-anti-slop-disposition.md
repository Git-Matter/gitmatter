Keep the current legal workstation direction. The useful work is the contract, its captured source,
the finding and the person's decision, within Client → Matter → artifact context. Warm monochrome
surfaces, Newsreader for public identity/legal reading, Inter for operations, compact tables and the
requested shadcn radius scale support that job. Another visual system would add risk to this demo.

interaction and state taxonomy. They are not 24 new defects, 24 independent shared-reference IDs,
or 24 current rendered passes. Use these records for the requested 20-plus correction accounting;
