## Customer outcome and demonstration

`PRODUCT.md` identifies Australian sole practitioners and general practices as the primary
audience. The immediate job is checking agreement obligations and preparing work the practitioner
can use and later explain. The product's distinction should be demonstrated through source,
decisions and history; no customer demand, time saving or legal-quality result is inferred.

## Confirmed strengths to preserve

- The current review route names flags, separates source identity from findings, gates actions on
  current review/completion data, and uses shared snapshot notices. This addresses the earlier
  perpetual-loading/stale-authority finding in source; runtime replay still matters.
- Guided instructions explicitly withhold inference until sending, distinguish jurisdiction from
  storage geography, and prohibit invented deadlines, correspondence delivery and self-approval.
  The practitioner panel requires exact state, visible content, scope and fresh human confirmation.
- Current public BYO-agent/key copy qualifies provider terms and retention. It explains the two
  execution routes without suggesting that an existing chat subscription funds application AI.
- The source-linked 29-pattern audit preserves useful typography, warm reading surfaces and
  functional boundaries. The two inspected AU mobile captures show readable inputs and explicit
  earlier-version/current-review separation. They are fictional component captures.
- The local structured receipt records default `vp test` passing 504 tests in 86 files, no skips,
  retries, worker or timeout overrides, at `04642c1`, with isolated Postgres/loopback MinIO. This is
  meaningful integration evidence, including the actual engine/audit paths, with bounded claims.
