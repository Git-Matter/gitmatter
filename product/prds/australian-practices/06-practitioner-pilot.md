# PRD 06: Practitioner pilot and evidence-based acceptance

## Problem and outcome

The target is still broad. We need observed work, repeat use, and willingness to pay from Australian sole practitioners and general practices before claiming product-market fit or time savings.

## First release

Provide a public pilot information/enquiry flow and an internal four-week research runbook. Aim to recruit five practitioners doing recurring document-bundle review and client correspondence. This is a recruitment target, not a statement that firms are participating.

## Requirements

1. Explain the intended participant, four-week structure, supported tasks, and how to enquire. Participation, pricing, and document handling are agreed individually.
2. Ask only for practice role/size, state or territory, recurring task, current tools, and preferred contact details. Tell applicants not to include client documents, names, confidential details, or credentials.
3. Enquiry is an explicit user action to the established contact address or booking page. Do not silently send mail, create CRM records, book meetings, or contact practitioners.
4. Prepare a baseline interview: observe the current task, checking, Word handoff, setup burden, and cost expectations. Obtain appropriate authorisation before using client material; offer fictional material for initial exploration.
5. Measure elapsed practitioner work including checking/corrections, material missed issues, unsupported assertions, Word failures, assistance required, second-matter use, and willingness to continue at an explicit AUD price.
6. Define a per-practitioner weekly evidence sheet. Never place client content in analytics or public reports.
7. Use predetermined go/change/stop gates: material confidentiality or authorisation failure stops affected testing; material output errors require correction and revalidation; repeat use and willingness to pay are recorded individually rather than replaced by satisfaction scores.

## Acceptance

- A visitor can understand and open an editable pilot enquiry without being enrolled or charged.
- The runbook contains screener, baseline script, weekly log template, outcome criteria, and an end-of-pilot decision process.
- Pilot dashboard/notes begin empty. No fabricated participants, testimonials, measurements, or adoption counts.
- Product-level browser and export evidence exists before inviting users to rely on the hosted workflow.

## Measurement and limits

Recruitment and observed legal work require real participants and separate scheduling. Completing code and materials does not complete the pilot. Public announcements, audience broadcasts, paid customer enrolment, and researcher outreach require explicit authorisation and appropriate operational readiness.
