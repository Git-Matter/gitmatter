# PRD 06: Practitioner pilot and evidence-based acceptance

## Problem and outcome

The target is still broad. We need observed work, repeat use, and willingness to pay from Australian sole practitioners and general practices before claiming product-market fit or time savings.

## Research scope

Maintain an internal four-week research runbook for recurring document-bundle review and client correspondence. The public pilot information and enquiry flow were removed on 17 November 2026. No participants or outcomes are established by these materials.

## Requirements

1. Prepare a baseline interview: observe the current task, checking, Word handoff, setup burden, and cost expectations. Obtain appropriate authorisation before using client material; offer fictional material for initial exploration.
2. Measure elapsed practitioner work including checking/corrections, material missed issues, unsupported assertions, Word failures, assistance required, second-matter use, and willingness to continue at an explicit AUD price.
3. Define a per-practitioner weekly evidence sheet. Never place client content in analytics or public reports.
4. Use predetermined go/change/stop gates: material confidentiality or authorisation failure stops affected testing; material output errors require correction and revalidation; repeat use and willingness to pay are recorded individually rather than replaced by satisfaction scores.

## Acceptance

- The runbook contains screener, baseline script, weekly log template, outcome criteria, and an end-of-pilot decision process.
- Pilot notes begin empty. No fabricated participants, testimonials, measurements, or adoption counts.
- Product-level browser and export evidence exists before inviting users to rely on the hosted workflow.

## Measurement and limits

Recruitment and observed legal work require real participants and separate scheduling. Completing code and materials does not complete the pilot or authorise outreach, meetings, or enrolment.
